# Masterclass collage

Selected direction: option 4, bold editorial paper collage. Built-in imagegen mode was used for the preview; the selected original remains in the generated-images library. The optimized project asset is `src/NextGenCoaching.Web/wwwroot/images/masterclass-collage.webp` (1448 × 1086).

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Preview hero artwork for NextGen Data Science AI cloud online masterclass page. Landscape 4:3. Bold sophisticated modern editorial cut-paper collage illustration, NOT 3D render, NOT photograph, NO large character portraits. Beautiful ivory and lilac background, strong cobalt blue blocks, coral cut-paper abstract silhouettes of two young adult students at laptops, oversized yellow cursor arrow, stylized analytical charts with thick bars, round AI chip symbol, cloud and database cutout shapes, playful hand-drawn connecting strokes and sparkles. A winding lime pathway joins the laptops to the data chart as a visual metaphor for discovering a tech career. Premium design studio aesthetic with paper grain, crisp layered geometric composition, clever negative space, striking graphic color combinations, elegant restrained shapes, genuinely different from neon cyberpunk or clay 3D. Complete main shapes within frame. Only small text 'AI', 'SQL' allowed, no headline, no fake numbers, no watermark.

## Implementation

The complete image is displayed without cropping. Motion animates SVG data packets over the art, highlights the AI node and draws a chart trace; paper accents float separately. The pause control, reduced-motion preference, offscreen state and page visibility govern playback. Light and dark themes share the original colorful art with different surrounding surfaces. The page retains server-backed session selection, registration and next-session enquiries, plus English, Marathi and Hindi copy. The image is illustrative, rather than a depiction of an actual class or student endorsement.
